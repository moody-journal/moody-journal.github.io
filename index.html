About Moody

A journal that reads between the lines

Most of us underestimate ourselves. We journal about our days without ever noticing the quiet moments of courage, kindness, and growth we've tucked into them. Moody changes that.

Write naturally. Discover yourself.

Moody is a mood-aware journal designed to help you slow down, check in with how you're feeling, and capture what's on your mind without pressure or prompts.

After you save, Moody's on-device AI reads your entry and surfaces the achievements hiding in plain sight: the time you reached out to a friend, repaid a favour, or simply got yourself outside. Small things. Real things.

📊 Mood tracking ✍️ Free-form journaling 🏅 Automatic achievements 🌬️ Guided breathing 📈 Mood trends

Awards that actually mean something

Moody doesn't hand out badges for streaks or step counts. Every award you earn is grounded in your own writing. One entry can earn several medals when it contains distinct achievements.

Whether you prioritised sleep, moved your body, connected with someone new, or simply stopped to notice something good, Moody sees it, names it, and reminds you it counts.

Guided Breathing

Arrive before you write

A slow, animated circle guides you through a breathing cycle before your journal opens. Take a quiet moment with a guided box-breathing cycle. The circle expands on the inhale, pauses, contracts on the exhale, and rests.

Privacy

Your words stay yours.

Moody was built with a simple principle: your most private thoughts should never leave your device.

🔒

100% on-device processing. All AI analysis in Moody is performed locally on your iPhone using Apple Intelligence. Your journal text is not sent to an AI server for analysis.

Apple Intelligence, on your device

Achievement detection runs on Apple's on-device Apple Intelligence models. Mood charts use the moods you log. Your writing is not sent to an external AI service.

No accounts, no tracking

Moody requires no account to use. There is no sign-in, no profile, no analytics tracking your behavior. What you write is stored only in your device's local storage and optionally backed up to your personal iCloud account.

iCloud backup, your terms

If you have iCloud Backup enabled, iOS may include Moody's local data in your personal device backup. This is governed entirely by you through iOS Settings. Moody itself never writes to any external storage independently.

No ads, no data sales

Moody has no advertising SDK, no analytics framework, and no third-party data sharing of any kind. The app generates no revenue from your data.
